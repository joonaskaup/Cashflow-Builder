Linked to a period or milestone

Fixed months or dates

Months clicked directly on the cost timeline and fixed payment dates stay where you chose them. Historical costs stay Historical.

Optional phase-percentage columns already in Erittely

Excel percentages are read as shares of the cost. Existing payment rules are retained; use “Review source phase split” in detailed options to review a source split for a selected cost.

Save and close the budget and extracted workbooks in Excel before updating them through the helper. Budget amounts are read from Excel’s saved calculations. Dates and payment edits are recalculated and written by the helper; an already-open Excel window does not reload a changed file automatically.

The original cashflow has 17 columns: when Historical is used, it occupies the first column and 16 monthly columns follow. Use Cashflow range to change the first month. Manual financing follows its existing calendar months; a change that would exclude financing is blocked. Amounts outside that display remain visible beside their costs.

Administration and contingency retain the source budget’s amounts. Their example payment rule is a forecast allowance during postproduction, not a record of actual payments.
