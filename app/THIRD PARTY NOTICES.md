# Included runtime libraries

This release contains CPython 3.12.14, openpyxl 3.1.5, et_xmlfile 2.0.0 and lxml 6.1.1. Their licenses are included with the runtime:

- Python: `runtime/python/lib/python3.12/LICENSE.txt`
- openpyxl: `runtime/python/lib/python3.12/site-packages/openpyxl-3.1.5.dist-info/LICENCE.rst`
- et_xmlfile: `runtime/python/lib/python3.12/site-packages/et_xmlfile-2.0.0.dist-info/LICENCE.rst` and `LICENCE.python`
- lxml and its bundled components: `runtime/python/lib/python3.12/site-packages/lxml-6.1.1.dist-info/licenses/`
